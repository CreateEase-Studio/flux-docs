FLUX PHOTOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CreateEase Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Flux Photos: AI Photo Gallery ("Flux Photos" or the "app"), Android package com.fluxphotos.android.

For privacy questions or requests, contact createease.studio@gmail.com.

Your library stays on your device. Ordinary gallery browsing and local tools do not automatically upload your entire library. Cloud AI tools send the photo and related inputs you select for processing. Account services, purchases, advertising, and usage analytics involve the information described below. Availability of these services depends on the app version, settings, region, and applicable consent requirements.

1. Photos, videos, and device access

With your Android permission, the app accesses photos, videos, and associated metadata to display and organize your library and provide albums, Memories, search, playback, editing, collages, compression, duplicate detection, and similar-photo tools. Metadata can include file names, folders, dates, file sizes, dimensions, media type, duration, and embedded camera or location information.

Embedded location information is read only where available and permitted. The app does not require continuous GPS tracking to provide its gallery features. Access to SD cards or USB storage is limited by the permissions or folders you grant through Android.

You can change or revoke media access in Android Settings. This may limit the files and features available in the app. Where Android requires confirmation to delete or modify a file, the app uses that system confirmation.

2. Local processing and Secure Vault

Thumbnails, library indexes, preferences, local recognition results, and editing data are stored on your device. Supported recognition tasks use on-device processing, including Google ML Kit. ML Kit may send technical usage and performance information to Google; its on-device recognition does not send your input photographs or recognition results to Google.

Face detection and facial landmarks may be used to align and preserve visible faces during editing. We do not use this functionality to identify people by name or build a facial-identification database.

Secure Vault stores media locally and restricts access inside Flux Photos using a PIN and, where enabled, Android biometric authentication. New Vault media copies are not separately encrypted by the app; older encrypted entries remain supported. A PIN is not a guarantee against access on a compromised device. Android handles biometric authentication; we do not receive or store your fingerprint or biometric authentication template.

Restore Vault files before uninstalling the app or clearing its data. App-private Vault files can be permanently lost when app data is removed. Device backup behavior depends on Android, your device settings, and the type of app data; do not rely on a device backup to recover Vault media.

3. Cloud AI tools

When you start a cloud AI operation, such as enhancement, restoration, colorization, object removal, background removal, or changing a scene, we process the selected image and the information needed for that operation. This may include a text instruction, editing mask, cutout, reference image where supported, image dimensions, and face-position or alignment information needed to preserve faces.

These inputs are transmitted to our Firebase/Google Cloud backend and to Replicate and the model services used to perform your request. Depending on the tool, those services include models provided by OpenAI, Google, or other model providers available through Replicate. Their service providers may process the inputs as necessary to return a result, operate their services, and enforce safety and abuse-prevention requirements.

We do not automatically submit every photo in your library, and we do not use your images to train our own AI models. Processing by external providers is subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. Upload only content you are entitled to share, including photographs of other people. AI output may alter details and should be reviewed before use.

See Replicate’s Privacy Policy, OpenAI’s Privacy Policy, and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Accounts, credits, and purchases

Firebase Authentication provides account functionality. An anonymous account identifier may be used for cloud tools. If you sign in with Google, we process the account identifier and profile information made available by Google, such as your email address, name, and profile picture. These help authenticate you and associate your credits and purchases with your account.

Google Play handles payments. We process purchase tokens, product and order identifiers, subscription status and expiry dates, credit balances, and transaction or verification records to validate purchases, deliver credits, restore access, and prevent fraud. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Google Play.

Uninstalling Flux Photos or requesting account deletion does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel it through Google Play subscriptions.

5. Advertising — Google AdMob

Flux Photos uses Google AdMob in versions and features where advertising is enabled. Google and its advertising partners process information to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. This can include your IP address and approximate location derived from it, advertising or other device identifiers, device and app information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic information.

Ad personalization depends on your consent, settings, age eligibility, region, and the available advertising configuration. Where required, a privacy message is presented before relevant advertising processing. You can review or withdraw consent through the privacy options made available with that advertising service and adjust advertising preferences in Android or your Google account. Non-personalized ads can still require information for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

We do not provide your gallery photos, videos, Vault files, AI image inputs, or editing prompts to AdMob for advertising targeting. Additional advertising partners, if used, are identified in the applicable consent information.

Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s advertising information.

6. Usage analytics — Microsoft Clarity

In versions where usage analytics is enabled, we use Microsoft Clarity to understand navigation, find usability problems, and improve the app. It processes device and app information, country or region, taps and gestures, errors, and interface layout. Clarity reconstructs session replays from collected interface and interaction data; its mobile SDK does not make a screen-video recording.

This information is transmitted to Microsoft. Analytics must be configured to exclude or mask private media, Vault content, editing inputs and results, credentials, and other sensitive information. Where consent is required, collection is subject to that consent. Contact us to request assistance with access or deletion of analytics data or to withdraw consent where applicable.

For more information, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Clarity mobile privacy guidance.

7. Technical information and support

Our backend and service providers process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, app or service versions, authentication and integrity checks, job status, errors, and security or transaction events. This is used to deliver the service, investigate failures, prevent abuse, and protect credits and purchases.

If you contact support or choose to export diagnostics, we receive the information you send. Please avoid including passwords, PINs, full payment details, or unnecessary private media.

8. Sharing and international processing

We disclose information to service providers only as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including Google/Firebase for hosting, authentication, storage, integrity and billing services; Replicate and relevant model services for AI processing; Google AdMob for advertising; and Microsoft Clarity for analytics where enabled.

We do not sell your gallery photos or videos. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or our legal rights, or in a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards and notice requirements. Sharing or exporting a file to another app is initiated by you and is subject to that app’s practices.

Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries with different privacy laws. Where applicable, processing is subject to the providers’ data-processing terms and legally required international-transfer safeguards. See Firebase’s privacy and security information.

9. Retention and security

  • Local files: Library originals remain in their storage locations until you change or delete them. Local app data and “My results” copies remain until removed by you, the app, or Android. Copies exported to your gallery remain until separately deleted.
  • Temporary cloud images: Our backend schedules cleanup of completed AI input and output files after approximately 24 hours. Storage lifecycle rules also target temporary images once they are at least one day old. Cleanup is asynchronous and can take longer during processing, retries, or service interruptions.
  • Account and service records: Account information, job metadata, credit balances, purchase records, and security logs are retained as needed to provide and protect the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Temporary image cleanup does not automatically delete all associated account or transaction records.
  • Provider records: External AI, advertising, analytics, and infrastructure providers apply their own retention policies. Our temporary-image cleanup does not guarantee simultaneous deletion from their systems or backups.

We use measures such as encrypted network connections, restricted backend access, authentication, and purchase verification. No storage system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your choices, rights, and deletion requests

You can manage Android permissions, choose which photos to submit to cloud AI, delete local results, control available advertising and analytics privacy options, and manage Google Play subscriptions separately.

To request access, correction, deletion of your Flux Photos account or associated server data, or assistance with a privacy choice, email createease.studio@gmail.com. For account deletion, use the subject “Flux Photos account deletion” and, where possible, send the request from the Google email linked to your account. We may ask for limited information to verify ownership. Do not send your password, Vault PIN, or full payment-card details.

Following verification, we delete the account and associated data unless limited retention is required for legal obligations, transaction reconciliation, fraud prevention, or resolving disputes. We explain relevant exceptions when responding. Remote account deletion does not erase files stored only on your device or automatically cancel a subscription.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to data portability, restriction of processing, objection, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to a data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Where applicable, we rely on performance of a contract to provide requested services, consent for optional processing where required, legitimate interests in reliability and security, and legal obligations for required records.

11. Children’s privacy

Flux Photos is not specifically directed to children. Children must not use account, purchase, advertising, or cloud AI features unless permitted by applicable age requirements and with parental involvement where required. If you believe we have received personal information from a child in violation of applicable law, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. A photo containing a child is not, by itself, an indication that the app user is a child.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version is identified by the date above. Where required, we provide additional notice or request consent before materially different processing begins.
